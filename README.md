## 👋 About me

I'm Alejandro, a 20-year-old Industrial Engineering student at the **[Universitat Politècnica de València (UPV)](https://www.upv.es/)**. Alongside my university studies, I dedicate time every day to learning Python independently, strengthening my technical foundations and building projects that help me put theory into practice.

My long-term goal is to connect the **real, operational side of industrial engineering** with the analytical possibilities of programming. I'm especially interested in how data can help us understand production systems, improve processes, monitor energy consumption, manage resources and make better inventory and demand-planning decisions.

I see **Python as a tool, not an end in itself**. The value is not simply writing code: it is understanding an engineering problem, asking the right questions, working carefully with data and turning the results into information that can support practical decisions.

I'm at the beginning of my professional journey, and I want this profile to reflect that honestly. The repositories here are active learning projects: a record of the concepts I'm studying, the problems I'm exploring and the progress I'm making over time.

## 🏭 Engineering mindset

I want to develop at the intersection of engineering, mathematics and data. The areas that currently motivate me most are:

- **Industrial operations:** production systems, process performance and operational analysis.
- **Data-driven engineering:** using data to understand what is happening in a process and why.
- **Energy and resources:** analysing consumption and exploring opportunities for greater efficiency.
- **Inventory and supply chains:** understanding stock levels, demand patterns and planning decisions.
- **Predictive modelling:** applying mathematical and statistical methods to estimate outcomes and support decisions.

These are areas I am actively learning about and aiming to explore through practical projects—not claims of professional specialisation.

## 🧠 How I learn and work

I believe that lasting technical knowledge comes from understanding the foundations, not just getting a program to run. My usual learning process is:

<div align="center">
  <strong>Concepts</strong> → <strong>Mathematics</strong> → <strong>Handwritten notes</strong> → <strong>Python implementation</strong> → <strong>Experiments</strong> → <strong>Conclusions</strong>
</div>

In practice, this means I try to:

- Understand the engineering or mathematical idea before implementing it.
- Derive and explain equations step by step whenever the subject requires it.
- Build small implementations from scratch to understand what happens behind the abstractions.
- Test ideas with data, review the results and document what I learn.
- Work consistently, take initiative and direct my effort towards clear learning goals.

I value **focused hard work**: being consistent matters, but so does choosing what to work on, understanding why it matters and learning from the result.

A personal knowledge base where I organise university and self-directed learning. It includes mathematical explanations, handwritten notes, Python notebooks and experiments covering topics such as Python, NumPy, Pandas and machine-learning foundations.

### [Industrial-Energy-Prediction](https://github.com/alejandrohernandezruiz/Industrial-Energy-Prediction) — Applying Python to an engineering problem

An engineering-oriented data project exploring how industrial operating conditions relate to energy consumption. It includes a data workflow and a **multiple linear regression model implemented from scratch** with NumPy, Pandas and Matplotlib.

The project uses a **synthetic dataset** representing industrial variables. It is a learning project designed to understand the mathematics and workflow behind a predictive model—not a deployed industrial forecasting system.

[![View repository](https://img.shields.io/badge/VIEW%20PROJECT-Industrial--Energy--Prediction-181717?style=flat-square&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/alejandrohernandezruiz/Industrial-Energy-Prediction)
